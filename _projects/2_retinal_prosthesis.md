*Senior Researcher · SURPASS Initiative, Johns Hopkins University ($2M)*

Degenerative retinal diseases destroy photoreceptors but often leave the retinal ganglion cells (RGCs) intact. If those cells can be stimulated precisely — without implanted electrode arrays — higher-resolution vision restoration becomes possible. I work on **photoacoustic stimulation**: light absorbed by a biocompatible polymer nanocomposite generates focused ultrasound that activates RGCs in a **non-contact, dynamically configurable** manner, with spatial resolution set by the acoustic focus rather than an electrode pitch.

This line of work was recognized with the **Robertson Translational Research Grant** at ARVO 2024, and the stimulation platform is now being characterized toward a high-resolution, configurable visual prosthesis.
